HangulSteps is for the moment right after you can read Hangul: letters → words → a sentence with the right particles → handwriting practice, all on one screen.

How it works

  • Sentences are built only from {{ counts.frames }} reviewed beginner frames and {{ counts.nouns }} nouns, {{ counts.verbs }} verbs and {{ counts.adjectives }} adjectives. Nothing is randomly combined.
  • Particle feedback separates the form rule (받침) from the naturalness rule (topic vs subject), so “grammatical but odd” is shown as exactly that.
  • Audio uses your device’s Korean voice (Web Speech API); nothing is sent anywhere. Your sentences are stored only in this browser.

Money

The site runs on ads and tutor referral links (we may earn a fee). Neither affects the feedback or the example sentences.
