What this checker does and does not judge

  • Judges: particle form (the 받침 rule), 저가 → 제가, 에 / 에서 / 을·를 against a recognised verb, detached particles, spacing of -고 싶어요 and 안, verb-last order, the polite ending.
  • Does not judge: meaning, naturalness, word choice, politeness level. Rules cannot decide those, so we do not pretend.
  • Words outside our list ({{ counts.nouns }} nouns, {{ counts.verbs }} verbs, {{ counts.adjectives }} adjectives) are shown with “?” and only their particle form is checked.
